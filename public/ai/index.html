Rapid Rims LLC

Rapid Rims LLC is a veteran-owned mobile wheel-repair company in the Las Vegas valley. We come to the driveway, apartment lot, or workplace. This is on-car cosmetic curb-rash repair, not a body shop and not a tire store named Rapid Tires.

What do you charge?

Light rash (1–2 spots on the lip) is $100 per rim. Heavier on-car work (grind, sand, polish or paint) is $125–$150 per rim. Two or more rims on the same visit are $90–$100 each. Military and veterans receive 10% off with ID. Cash preferred; card can be arranged. Quote is from photos.

How long does it take?

Most repairs stay on the car. About 20 minutes per rim. A typical set of four is about an hour.

Where do you work?

Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Spring Valley, Enterprise, and nearby. Evenings and weekends. Text (612) 219-5065. Email rapidrimsllc@gmail.com.
